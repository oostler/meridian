# Meridian

Learn the world on a 3D globe: every country, the 50 US states, Canada's provinces and territories,
China's provinces and regions, and the five oceans. Each place has a short history anchored on the
20th century and a snapshot of its culture, politics and leaders today.

**Play it:** https://oostler.github.io/meridian/

Works on phones, tablets and desktops, and offline after the first visit (add it to your home screen
to use it like an app). Built from https://github.com/oostler/claude-learn (`atlas/`).

Map data: Natural Earth (public domain), mledoze/world-countries (ODbL), OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
Basemap tiles © Esri and its data providers. Globe rendering: MapLibre GL JS (BSD-3-Clause).
