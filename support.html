Support

Spotted a mistake?

Every map and story has a "Report an error" link. You can also send feedback here.

Your progress

Progress is saved on your device. To start over, open Settings and choose "Reset all progress". Clearing your browser or app data also removes it.

Deep dives

Meridian Plus unlocks every deep dive on this device. If you have an access code, enter it on the Deep dives screen.

Offline

Meridian works offline once loaded, and map areas you have already seen stay available.
