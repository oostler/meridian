Support

Spotted a mistake?

Every map and story has a "Report an error" link. It opens an email to us, ready to send. You can also write to support@globegenius.io.

Your profile

Sign in with Google or with your email from the Compete tab. To change your name, sign out or delete your profile, open Settings. Deleting your profile removes your name, scores and sign-in account. Forgot your password? Choose "Continue with email", then "Forgot password?".

Your progress

Learning progress is saved on your device. To start over, open Settings and choose "Reset all progress". Clearing your browser or app data also removes it.

Plus and donations

On the website, Plus (deep dives, every country's full history, stats, private leagues and streak repair) is a thank-you for funding a malaria net: any gift of $5 or more to the Against Malaria Foundation through our Every.org fundraiser unlocks it for a year. In the iPhone app, Plus is free. We never receive any of the money. Receipts, refunds and cancelling a monthly gift are handled by Every.org from your Every.org account.

Offline

Globe Genius works offline once loaded, and map areas you have already seen stay available. Leaderboards and races need a connection.

Contact

Privacy requests and anything else: support@globegenius.io.
