Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-24

The short version

  • You can use Meridian without a profile. Your progress then stays on your device.
  • If you make a profile, we store your first name, your email and your scores so you can appear on the leaderboards and race friends.
  • Your first name, a four-digit tag and your scores are public to other Meridian players. Your email is never shown.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking across apps or websites, and we never sell or share your data for advertising.
  • You can delete your profile at any time in Settings.

Who we are

Meridian is an educational geography and history app made by an independent developer ("we"). We are responsible for the personal data described here.

Using Meridian without a profile

Your learning progress, streak, quiz scores, daily goal and settings are saved in your browser or app storage on your own device and are not sent to us. Clearing the app's data, or "Reset all progress" in Settings, removes them.

What a profile stores

  • Sign-in details: your email address and, if you sign in with Google or Apple, the name and account identifier that service shares. If you use a password, it is handled by our sign-in provider and we never see it.
  • Profile: your first name and a random four-digit tag (for example "Sam #4821").
  • Scores: points earned this week and your world-knowledge score, a percentage worked out on your device from what you have mastered.
  • Races: while you race, your name, tag, answers, answer times and score are shared with the other players in that race. When the host leaves, the race room is deleted. A room abandoned before the host leaves may stay on the server; it holds only those details.
  • Technical records: our providers keep standard records such as sign-in times and IP addresses to run and secure the service.

Why we use it

Only to run Meridian: to sign you in, show the leaderboards, run races and keep the service secure. Where data protection law asks for a legal basis, this is to provide the service you asked for and our legitimate interest in keeping it working and safe.

What is public

Your first name, tag, weekly points and world-knowledge score appear on the leaderboards, which every signed-in player can see. Use a name you are happy to show. Your email address and sign-in details are never shown.

Who processes it

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Realtime Database) stores profiles, scores and races, in the United States. Firebase privacy information.
  • Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple, if you choose them, under Google's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.
  • Esri (ArcGIS) supplies map images and receives your IP address and the map area requested. Esri's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts supplies the app's typeface and receives your IP address.
  • GitHub hosts the website and receives standard request records. "Report an error" opens a public GitHub issue that you choose to submit, under GitHub's privacy statement; don't include personal details there.
  • Apple (and Google Play) handle any in-app purchase. We never see your card or payment details.

Because these providers are based in the United States, your data may be processed outside your country. They protect it under their own safeguards and contracts.

How long we keep it

We keep your profile and scores until you delete your profile. Weekly points reset each week. Deleting your profile removes your profile, scores and sign-in account straight away; our providers may keep routine logs for a limited time.

Your choices and rights

You can change your first name, sign out, or delete your profile in Settings at any time. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or California law), you may also have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to your local data protection authority. To exercise these rights, contact us as below.

Children

Anyone can learn with Meridian without a profile. Profiles are for people aged 13 or over (or the minimum age in your country, such as 16 in parts of Europe). If you believe a child under that age has made a profile, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and access rules let each player change only their own profile and race answers.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the contact details on our Support page. You can delete your profile yourself at any time in Settings.
